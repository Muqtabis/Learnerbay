2. Cookies & Google AdSense

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to this site and/or other sites across the web.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or review How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You may also disable cookies in your browser settings.

3. Technical Logs & External Links

Static hosting providers may record standard server access logs (such as IP address, browser type, and timestamp) to maintain availability and security. External educational websites linked from our roadmaps and resource table maintain their own privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Learners B.A.Y is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
